at the rear screen edge stop descending and connect directly upward to the
raised clip bridge with one straight top plane. Add material above the current
screen sidewalls and front bridge, retaining underside, open screen aperture,
clip axis/bore and both printing planes. User requested a preview only.

Y=-192.950417, Z=56.128437 mm, an 11.26899° incline toward the front.
Green on the before/after sheet marks this new top. The 86 mm-wide plan aperture
remains open between its current rear and front upper edges. Existing lower
screen-front incline is retained beneath the new material. No fillets.

Prototype remains one valid native solid, adding approximately 37379.7578 mm³
and removing zero original material (native Boolean difference checked).
Original printing-plane half-spaces are reapplied to additions. New material is
behind the clip bore tangent, so the clip bore is not entered.
